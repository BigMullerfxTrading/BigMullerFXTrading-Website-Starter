ABOUT BIG MULLERFX

Built on Price Action. Driven by Discipline.

BIG MULLERFX represents a disciplined approach to Forex trading built around one core strategy: Price Action. The objective is to understand market direction, study price behaviour, identify meaningful areas of interest, and wait patiently for a valid trading opportunity.

Market structure helps establish directional context. Supply and Demand, Fair Value Gaps, Support and Resistance, liquidity, and Fibonacci levels act as supporting confluences rather than replacing the core strategy.

Every setup must make sense within the broader analysis. I avoid chasing price, forcing entries, and reacting emotionally to losses. Patience and disciplined execution remain essential parts of my trading process.

My approach prioritises planned risk, candle-close confirmation, and opportunities offering a minimum planned risk-to-reward ratio of 1:3.

THE BIG MULLERFX TRADING MODEL

Price Action Is the Foundation

A structured trading process combining directional analysis, supporting confluences, confirmation, and disciplined risk management.

01

Price Action & Market Structure

Price Action is the foundation of my trading model. I analyse trends, market behaviour, Break of Structure (BOS), and Change of Character (CHOCH) to understand directional bias and identify potential opportunities.

02

Supply & Demand

Supply and Demand zones support my Price Action analysis. I pay attention to how price approaches these areas and whether its behaviour supports the overall trading idea.

03

Points of Interest

I assess relevant Points of Interest across monthly, weekly, and daily market context. These may include Fair Value Gaps (FVGs), Supply and Demand zones, Support and Resistance, and relevant trendline areas.

04

Liquidity Analysis

I consider internal liquidity and external liquidity, including monthly, weekly, and daily highs and lows. Liquidity helps provide context for potential price reactions and trading opportunities.

05

Fibonacci & CRT

Fibonacci premium and discount analysis uses the 50%, 61.8%, and 78.6% levels where relevant. CRT is considered when it aligns with my marked zone and the broader Price Action analysis.

06

Confirmation & Execution

I wait for candle-close confirmation before committing to a setup. The goal is to execute based on the conditions of my trading model rather than impatience, speculation, or emotional reactions.

07

Risk-to-Reward

My trading plan targets a minimum planned risk-to-reward ratio of 1:3. Risk is considered before entry, with capital protection and consistent execution taking priority over chasing profits.

08

Trading Discipline

No FOMO. No revenge trading. No forced setups. I focus on patience, preparation, and following my own trading model. Not every opportunity needs to be traded.

09

Continuous Improvement

Every trading decision is an opportunity to learn. Reviewing execution, respecting the plan, and developing consistency are more important than making unrealistic promises about results.
